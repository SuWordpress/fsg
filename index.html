FAQ

Common Questions

Are your quotes and inspections really free?

Yes. A full roof inspection costs €0. We'll look at the job and give you a clear, fixed price with no obligation to go ahead.

How quickly can you come out?

For most jobs we can call out within a few days. For urgent leaks or storm damage, call us and we'll get to you as fast as we can.

Do I need to replace my fascia and soffits, or can they be repaired?

Small areas of damage can often be repaired. If the timber behind is rotten, replacing with UPVC or aluminium is usually the better long-term option. We'll always tell you honestly.

How often should gutters be cleaned?

Usually once a year, ideally after autumn when leaves have fallen. Homes near trees may need it twice a year.

Which areas do you cover?

Every town in County Cork and County Kerry.
